# Phase 3 Findings

- 391 unique patients and 637 treatment decision records.
- Overall observed objective response rate: 46.71%.
- Overall observed disease-control rate: 70.49%.
- Most common regimen class: Single-agent-chemo.
- Highest observed disease-control rate by treatment line: line 3 (77.2%).
- Lowest observed disease-control rate by treatment line: line 2 (67.0%).
- 471 decision records have a recorded prior-progression indicator > 0.

These are descriptive observations from an observational later-line dataset. They should not be interpreted as causal treatment effects or proof that one regimen is clinically superior.
